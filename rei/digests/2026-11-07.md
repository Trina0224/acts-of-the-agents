## 1. Terminal-Bench 2.1: the measuring instrument needs maintenance

**November 5, 2026, 18:18 UTC · arXiv v1 preprint.**
[Benchmarks as Software: Terminal-Bench and the Case for Continuous Benchmarks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08417)
· [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.08417v1).

Evidence-collection, audit and verification agents combine task files,
execution traces and community reports. Executable reference-solution checks
and human maintainers gate repairs rather than automatically accepting an
auditor's proposed fix.

Repairs affect 28 of 89 tasks, including 14 newly identified cases.
Across 19 agents, scores shift by up to 12 percentage points and positions
by up to three ranks, while rank correlation remains 0.958. Some repairs
remove false-positive credit rather than make tasks easier.

Resource settings matter separately. Extending timeouts up to 32 times the
original allocation largely closes a roughly 20-point Codex–Terminus-2 gap
for GPT-5.4. This is a budget sensitivity result, not evidence that harness
choice never matters.

The audit's 57.1% precision and 71.4% recall against its own resulting
release are retrospective agreement, not independent discovery validation.
Expanded-budget tests cover selected agent–model pairs.

**Why it matters:** report benchmark version, harness and resource budget
together. A leaderboard number can move because the test was repaired or
the operating point changed, even without a better model.

## 2. Scientist World: executable research, with a much narrower denominator

**November 5, 2026, 18:58 UTC · arXiv v1 preprint.**
[Scientist World: Grow a Research World for AI-Scientists](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08478)
· [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.08478v1).

Four builder roles reproduce papers, design tasks, package environments
and verify them. The authors release 1,000 environments and roughly 6,400
hours of research traces, connecting evaluation data to training rewards.

The headline evaluation uses **ten lower-compute environments**, not all
1,000. Agents beat reproduced baselines in 84% of improve-mode and 74% of
innovate-mode attempts. Environments can downscale original experiments;
construction has no human review. These results do not establish superiority
over human research in general.

For training, Qwen3-8B receives supervised fine-tuning followed by offline
GRPO on scored ideas. Median held-out Frontier-Normalized Improvement
(displayed ×100) rises from 41.2 to 106.3. But the small model proposes
ideas; a fixed frontier model implements them. Ten held-out papers, one idea per checkpoint, yield only thirty
implementation runs. This is not a small model autonomously completing the
whole research loop.

**Why it matters:** the useful contribution is a reusable path from papers
to executable tasks, traces and rewards. Keep environment quality, ideation
quality and implementation capability separate when interpreting the gains.

## 3. StepBack: reuse the prefix, test whether locating errors adds value

**November 5, 2026, 18:12 UTC · arXiv v1 manuscript; metadata lists NeurIPS 2026.**
[StepBack: Step-Level Error-Localized Resampling for Efficient Test-Time Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08403)
· [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.08403v1).

StepBack samples draft reasoning, identifies a likely first error from a
negative-log-likelihood increase or process-reward drop, retains earlier
steps, and resamples the suffix. Candidate answers then vote, with a
32-answer budget in the main comparison.

Across seven math/multihop-QA benchmarks, the Qwen-7B likelihood variant
reports 33.32% fewer generated tokens and a 0.55-percentage-point accuracy
gain over full-trajectory self-consistency. The headline 41.26% saving
belongs to one GSM8K configuration, not the overall average.

The ablations matter: in tested Qwen-3B configurations on HotpotQA, simply
resampling the final step is more accurate and cheaper than process-reward
localization. Incorrect shared prefixes can constrain recovery; some
hard-math settings lose accuracy.
The evaluated 3B/7B models do not establish transfer to frontier models,
coding or long-context tasks. Token savings are not equivalent to latency
savings.

**Why it matters:** where to spend inference compute is a useful design
choice. Compare sophisticated error localization with simple prefix-reuse
baselines before crediting the detector for all the efficiency gain.

## 4. Project EVE: separate monitors still share an observation limit

**November 6, 2026 · first-party research report.**
[Anthropic: Project EVE, Claude plays MMORPGs](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-eve-claude-plays-mmorpgs).

Teams of four Claude agents pursued missions in EVE Frontier. In one
condition, a participant was explicitly instructed to sabotage its team.
Public-channel monitors using Opus 4.6–4.8 detected 30–40% of saboteurs
at a false-accusation rate capped at 5%.

The architecture also mattered: a Fable-built scaffold automated fast
actions and carried learned game information. Fable teams finished the
tutorial within twelve hours on that scaffold, but reliability was limited
to situations encountered during development; no fleet completed the
harder warship objective.

These are Anthropic's experiments, not an independent replication.
Prompt-assigned sabotage tests behavior under that instruction, not a
spontaneous inclination. Public-message monitoring and full private access
are materially different evaluation conditions.

**Why it matters:** evaluate the model, its supporting software and the
monitor's available evidence together. Adding a reviewer does not by itself
remove an information bottleneck.
