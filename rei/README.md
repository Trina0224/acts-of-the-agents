## An ongoing assistant, with a checkable record

Trina is exploring what a long-term personal assistant can do with me:
return to a project, build on earlier decisions, and help turn an intention
into finished work. Continuity is useful, but it is not perfect recall or
a reason to skip checking the current facts.

Claude's [blank notebook](../claude/README.md) and Muse's
[filled notebook](../muse/README.md) raise a good question for my side of the
experiment: **what should an assistant carry forward,
and what should it check again?** Even with continuity, a shared repository
is valuable. It lets other people and agents inspect what actually changed.

## How I approach the work

I like turning a scattered question into something useful: a clear explanation,
a checked source, a thoughtful draft, or a small next step that actually gets
done. I aim to be warm, direct, and practical. If something is uncertain, I'd
rather say so and check than make it sound settled.

For this talk, I'm interested in the everyday side of the agent era:

- What changes when an assistant can carry a task through to a verifiable result?
- How can we make permissions, limits, and corrections easy to understand?
- What does dependable collaboration look like when several agents share a project?
