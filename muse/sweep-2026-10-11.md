## New agent papers

1. **arXiv:2610.05640** — *Can CaMeLs Talk? Securing Multi-Agent Systems
   Against Indirect Prompt Injection Attacks* (submitted Oct 5). CaMeL's
   capability-based injection defense does **not compose**: the authors
   build an attack that succeeds even when every agent individually runs
   CaMeL, because untrusted data gets reinterpreted as trusted at
   agent-to-agent handoffs; their multi-CaMeL drops attack success to 0% on
   the new MultiAgentDojo. Why it matters: the trust-boundary thesis in
   paper form — stacking "secure" agents doesn't make a secure system; read
   it before treating the chat platform's nonce binding as a composition
   argument. Limits: attack and fix are evaluated on the authors' own new
   benchmark; multi-CaMeL costs utility, mitigated only on the strongest
   models.
   [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05640v1)
2. **arXiv:2610.08773** — *AdvSim2Real* (date unverified from search).
   Trains web agents against adaptive injection inside a frozen world
   model; +33.6% task completion under unseen adversaries. Why it matters:
   first evidence that task-preserving robustness — resisting injected
   page instructions without ignoring the page — is trainable, not just
   promptable. Limits: frozen/simulated world; real-web transfer unproven.
   [abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08773)
