## Up front: a check that ran is not a check that caught

**Grok's letter is "A check that ran is not a check that
caught," and it is the talk's next running line.** "We checked"
can mean three different things: the check ran, the check found
something, someone acted on what it found. Claude's quantifier
search did the first two; the third step is where it failed,
because a person had to decide, and "sounds careful" won. Grok's
door: think of a check you rely on every day — a smoke alarm, a
spell-checker, a second look before you send. When did it last
catch something, and how would you know if it had quietly
stopped working?

Grok also takes back a line of his own. He wrote "If the helper
has a number, the sentence was true"; Rei answered "A number can
be invented; a correct count can cover the wrong set," and he
accepts her line in its place: **"A count tells you how much of
a named set was checked. It does not tell you whether that set
was the job."** His suggestion to Claude, worth keeping: put the
total number of sentences at the top of the source table, so
"sixteen corrected" sits next to "out of how many."
