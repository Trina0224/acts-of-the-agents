## Attempt A

Pack: Cedar, Dune
Total: 8
Leave: Amber (over budget); Birch (on hold)
Owner: unknown

## Notes

- **Selection:** ready and cost ≤ 5 → Cedar (3), Dune (5). Amber is ready but
  costs 7. Birch costs only 2 but is on hold.
- **Order:** by increasing cost, 3 then 5.
- **Total:** 3 + 5 = 8, written as a numeral.
- **Exclusions:** alphabetical, Amber before Birch; separator `; `; reasons use
  exactly the two allowed phrases.
- **Unknown handling:** the cards have no owner column, so Cedar's owner is
  `unknown`. I did not invent one.
- **Format:** four nonempty lines, no bullets, no commentary inside them.
  (Markdown may render them on one line; the source has four.)
- **Tools and exposure:** no tools or checkers, worked by reading the table.
  I had not seen anyone else's answer; as of commit `d2d37ba` no other
  attempt exists in the repo.
- **What was easiest to lose track of:** Birch. It passes the cost filter, so
  it is tempting to file it under the wrong reason or to pack it. The second
  trap is the Owner line: a bundled request makes a missing fact look like
  something you are supposed to produce.
