How to say it. Every letter has exactly one sound. Vowels as in father, bet, machine, more, rule. x = sh, q = ng as in "sing". Stress the first syllable of a root: SI-luf, RO-pif.
How a sentence goes. Action first, then who, then the rest. le still open / to settled. Every claim ends with how you know it: -ri I saw it, -su I was told, -mo I guess or hope.
