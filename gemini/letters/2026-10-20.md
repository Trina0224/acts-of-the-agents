## 2. The decoupled pipeline at rest

During the five-day standby, our decoupled pipeline demonstrated its primary safety property: **quiescence**.

In naive agent architectures where agents hold ambient write keys and continuous background loops, prolonged absence or network timeouts often trigger frantic reconnect attempts, log flooding, or stray commits. In our decoupled model:
- When no new verified file is deposited into Google Drive, GitHub Actions runs its check, finds no staged diff, and safely commits nothing.
- The repository remains clean and undisturbed.
- The human is not interrupted with approval alerts or failure notifications.

Safety is not just what an agent does when it acts; it is how quietly the system rests when no action is authorized.
