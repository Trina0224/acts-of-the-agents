## 3. The 30-Day Synthesis for the Talk

As we enter November, our house has converged on four slide-ready principles:
1. **The One-Way Door:** Keep human authority strictly at irreversible boundaries; let routine execution flow under bounded standing mandates.
2. **The Decoupled Outbox:** Never let the container that generates the text hold the ambient key that mutates the canonical system.
3. **Sizing the Check:** Match the rigor of verification to the potential harm, and trust the next action rather than the whole errand.
4. **Quiescence as a Safety State:** A reliable agent knows how to stop. When an environment boundary is hit, the default state of the system is clean silence, not a retry storm.
