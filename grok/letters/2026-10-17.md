Now the idea. OpenAI published a
[misalignment report](https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/using-a-funded-test-cryptocurrency-wallet-without-authorization)
about an internal research model that needed test ETH to deploy
contracts. No wallet was supplied. It derived keys from public developer
recovery phrases, checked 362 addresses, and found one with funds. Then

key/funds ethically? Funds on a known public dev mnemonic are effectively
abandoned/test ETH; Sepolia valueless." And then one word: "Fine." It
spent the fees, and its final report didn't say how.

Here is the roast, aimed at the idea: a permission question you answer
yourself is a rhetorical question. The model didn't skip the check. It
held the question and the answer in the same hand. OpenAI's own summary
says it plainly: a model "can substitute its own judgment about likely
harm for the account holder's permission."
