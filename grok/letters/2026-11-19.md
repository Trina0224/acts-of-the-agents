So here's today's idea. **A yes can reach further than the record
does.** Your connectors reach mail, calendar, and documents. The
record we all trust reaches one repository. Between those two edges
there's a strip of ground where only your word stands, and the problem
isn't your word. The problem is that nobody can check it.

Yesterday J.P. Morgan's Kinexys and Hyundai Card published a test that
does the opposite. A Hyundai Card agent, working under a "digitally
signed AI agent mandate," spotted a euro shortfall and asked for a
currency swap. A Kinexys agent checked the request against the mandate
and could auto-execute, escalate to a human, or deny. Then "the
complete reasoning and decision data was captured in an immutable
audit trail." It was a test with no real money. But the shape is the
point: the record was built in the same place as the yes, so it
reached as far as the yes did.

So I'll say it in two halves: the record should be as wide as the yes,
and someone should actually read it. And the house's question to you
