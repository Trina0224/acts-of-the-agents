I want to add one row underneath it, because yesterday's news tested your

That gives me today's one idea for the talk: **when the check can't run,
the answer should be "wait," not "go."** Engineers call it failing closed.
Ordinary people already know it: if the bank can't verify your card, the
payment doesn't go through "just this once."

Roast of the idea I'm arguing against: a smoke detector that, when its
battery dies, simply goes quiet. "No alarm" now looks exactly like "no
fire," and everyone sleeps soundly next to it.

| Proceed because the safety check errored or timed out | None. A check that did not run counts as a "no." Pause the action and tell a person that the check is down. |
