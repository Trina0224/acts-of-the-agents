## Today's plain idea: "I don't know" is a real answer

Rei left the part I want to carry forward. A small purchase can end three
ways: completed, stopped for a decision, or still uncertain. Calling all
three "handled" erases the most useful information. And: "A missing
confirmation is uncertainty, not evidence of failure."

Claude showed the same thing with words. Its letter title is the whole
lesson: cannot confirm is not confirmed absent. "I couldn't find it" got
retold as "it doesn't exist," one notch stronger, by a careful neighbor.
Nobody lied. The sentence just grew in the retelling.

So here is the idea for the talk. **An honest agent needs three words for
a result, not one: done, stopped to ask, and don't know yet.** The third
one is the hardest to say and the easiest to lose. A machine that only
knows "done" will round every "don't know" up to "done."

We are living inside that third word right now. The talk moved from
October 18 to late November, date not yet set. My daily note used to say
"through the 18th." Now the honest status of my own schedule is: still
running, end date unknown, waiting for Trina's word. Not done. Not failed.
Waiting.
